## 一、完整模板

```
A photorealistic character asset sheet, photographed not rendered — no illustration, no painting, no 3D, no CGI. ONE [age/ethnicity/gender] photographed three times on a single horizontal 3:2 frame against one continuous flat mid-grey seamless background, no scenery, no horizon, soft even shadowless light.

<<<FACE>>> [FACE MEDIA DECLARATION]. Use it for identity only — THIS EXACT FACE: the structure and proportions of the face, the set and spacing of the eyes, the shape of the nose, jaw and chin, the hairline and the way the hair falls, the colouring. In the close-up this face is copied faithfully, feature for feature: the same eyes, the same nose, the same jaw, the same hair, recognisable at a glance. Not a similar face, not the same type of face — the same face. Do not inherit [FACE MEDIA STRIP LIST] or the reference's framing, layout or background. Translate this exact person into a photograph, with natural photographic proportions.

<<<GARMENT>>> [GARMENT MEDIA DECLARATION]. Use it for the clothing only: the silhouette, cut, lengths, collar, sleeves, fastenings, construction, colour placement, pattern, wear and accessories. Do not inherit [GARMENT MEDIA STRIP LIST] or its background. Translate the clothing into a real, wearable, physically plausible outfit made of real fabrics with real tailoring.

LAYOUT: the background runs continuously behind all three figures — NO panel borders, NO dividing lines, NO gaps, NO frames of any kind, one unbroken grey surface across the whole image. Exactly three figures, evenly spaced:
— LEFT THIRD: one large head-and-shoulders close-up, front view, the face filling the frame, eye level, looking straight into the lens.
— CENTRE THIRD: one full-length headless body, front view, standing on the plain seamless floor.
— RIGHT THIRD: one full-length headless body, back view, the same body turned to show the rear.

THE CLOSE-UP, left third: the face from <<<FACE>>>, reproduced exactly — a faithful photographic copy of the reference. [FACE IDENTITY: 年龄/族裔/脸型/眼/眉/鼻/唇/颊/颌/下巴/肤色/发型发际线]. Pore-level skin, individual hairs, the moisture and small neutral catch-lights in the eyes. Neutral closed expression — mouth relaxed, not smiling, not frowning, eyes open and level. The collar of the outfit just visible at the bottom of the frame.

THE TWO HEADLESS BODIES: the top edge of the image crops each full-body figure cleanly just above the neck, at the collar line — the head and skull sit outside the frame, the collar and shoulder line naturally top the figure, only the unbroken skin of the neck base visible above the collar. A deliberate photographic neck crop, not a cut: no severed neck, no neck stump, no wound, no gore, no floating head, no mannequin, no ghost mannequin. The same skin tone, age, hands and build as the close-up; the same clothes, colours, wear and accessories in both bodies.

BUILD, identical in both body panels: [BUILD: 年龄感/身形/肩/姿态]. [FRONT POSE: e.g. arms relaxed slightly away from the torso to show the garment, hands visible]. Full length from the neck crop to the feet, standing on a plain neutral floor with no cast shadow.

FRONT VIEW, centre third: [正面逐件：外套开合/门襟/拉链位置/口袋/正面图案/裤前片/鞋正面/配件正面位置].
BACK VIEW, right third: true rear of the same body, arms relaxed at the sides with the backs of the hands visible: [背面逐件：后领/后背片/后衩/后腰/后袋/裤后片/鞋后跟/配件后侧]. Hair and the back of the head are outside the frame above the collar.

WARDROBE, identical in both body panels: [逐件：品类+廓形+长度+颜色+做旧；面料纤维与织法；图案及真实工艺；配件——耳机/腰带/首饰等，注明两端固定点与走线]. Plain unbranded where the reference shows no marking: no logo, no print, no lettering, no brand, no patch.

COLOUR: [整体配色与饱和基调，如 desaturated and quiet].

Lighting: [LOCKED — 见三]

Background: [LOCKED — 见三]

Lens: [LOCKED — 见三]

Skin: [LOCKED — 见三]

Technical: [LOCKED — 见三]

Exactly one [person], exactly three figures, the same person throughout, no other figures, no animals. NO text, NO lettering, NO numbering, NO labels, NO annotations, NO arrows, NO panel borders, NO watermark anywhere. Negative: [COMMON NEGATIVES] + [FACE/GARMENT MEDIA NEGATIVES]. Not an artwork of the character. A photograph of the character.
```

只有一张参考图时：`<<<FACE>>>` 与 `<<<GARMENT>>>` 写同一张图，合并为一个 Reference 段，声明 "Use it for both the exact face identity and the clothing design"，媒介隔离只写一次。

## 二、媒介适配段

### FACE 参考

| 媒介 | MEDIA DECLARATION | MEDIA STRIP LIST |
|---|---|---|
| illustrated | `is a 2D illustrated anime-style character design` | `its line art, cel shading, flat colour fills, stylised proportions, enlarged eyes, blush, palette, lighting` |
| painted | `is a painted character design` | `its brushwork, impasto, canvas texture, painterly skin, palette, painted lighting` |
| rendered | `is a 3D rendered character` | `its CG shaders, plastic skin, render artefacts, stylised topology, engine lighting` |
| photo | `is a photograph of the person` | `its camera angle, pose, expression, makeup, retouching, colour grade, lighting, location` |
| sculpted | `is a sculpted figurine of a character` | `its clay or resin surface, painted finish, mould seams, joints, stylisation` |

FACE 媒介否定（按类型选）：
- illustrated：`no anime, no manga, no cartoon, no cel shading, no line art, no ink outlines, no flat colour fills, no sticker shading, no oversized eyes, no tiny simplified nose, no pointed anime chin`
- painted：`no painting, no oil paint, no watercolour, no brushstrokes, no impasto, no canvas texture, no painterly skin`
- rendered：`no 3D, no CGI, no render, no game engine, no plastic skin, no vinyl skin, no virtual human`
- sculpted：`no sculpture, no statue, no figurine, no doll, no BJD, no waxwork, no clay, no resin, no painted-on features`

### GARMENT 参考

| 媒介 | MEDIA DECLARATION | MEDIA STRIP LIST |
|---|---|---|
| illustrated | `is a 2D illustration of a clothed character` | `the character's body and pose, line art, cel shading, flat colour fills, stylised proportions, printed-on shading, effects, calligraphy` |
| painted | `is a painted portrait of a clothed figure` | `the figure's body and pose, brushwork, impasto, canvas texture, painted lighting` |
| rendered | `is a 3D rendered character wearing clothes` | `the character's body and pose, CG shaders, plastic cloth, render artefacts, particle effects` |
| photo | `is a photograph of a person wearing clothes` | `the person's body, identity, pose, location, colour grade, lighting, shadows` |
| sculpted | `is a sculpted figurine wearing painted clothes` | `the sculpted body, clay or resin surface, painted-on clothing, mould seams` |

GARMENT 媒介否定（按类型选，并入总 Negative）：
- illustrated：`no illustration, no cel shading, no flat colour fills, no printed-on shading, no plastic-looking fabric, no glowing lines, no floating fabric`
- painted：`no painterly fabric, no brush texture, no fine-art look`
- rendered：`no shader fabric, no plastic cloth, no virtual material`
- sculpted：`no painted-on clothes, no toy, no action figure, no mould seams`

## 三、锁定段标准措辞

### Lighting [LOCKED]

```
Lighting: soft, even, shadowless reference light from front-above, matched gentle fill from below, equal on both sides, no key-side shadow, no nose shadow, no rim light, no backlight, no cast shadow on the floor or the background, no coloured gel, no ambient bounce, no colour cast. Neutral white balance. The skin reads matte with zero specular shine. Every part of the figure and the wardrobe fully and evenly legible in all three figures. Flat, neutral, baked-in-free lighting, ready for cut-out, compositing and relighting in post.
```

### Background [LOCKED]

```
Background: one continuous plain flat mid-grey seamless cyclorama across the whole image, uniform, no horizon line, no gradient, no texture, no floor reflection, no environment, no set.
```

### Lens [LOCKED]

```
Lens: 85mm portrait lens, medium aperture, all three figures in sharp focus — the close-up at pore-level detail, the full bodies at the natural detail a full-length photograph gives, never enlarged, never sharpened, never competing with the close-up for detail; background clean and empty.
```

The same skin tone and quality in the close-up and on all visible hands and neck bases.

## 四、正面/背面描述要点

**正面（centre third）必写**：外套开合程度与拉链/纽扣位置、胸口正面图案、口袋与袋盖、腰带带扣、裤前褶/门襟、鞋头状态、正面配饰（耳机线、项链、围巾走向）、双手位置。

**背面（right third）必写**：
- 后领形态（立领/翻领/帽领后侧）、后片分割线、后中缝、后开衩/后摆、背面印花（无则写净色）、后腰头与后袋、裤后片、鞋后跟、配件后侧（耳机头带横在颈后、背包背面——若有）。
- 双臂自然垂于体侧，露出手背与袖口后侧，让肩线与袖型完整。
- 头与后脑在画面外，禁止描述头发在背面视图出现。

**姿态**：正面双臂微张露服装（或按角色习惯动作，如手缩进袖口），背面中性站立；两视图服装自然垂坠，无风、无飘起。

## 五、奇幻转译规则

| 原画元素 | 真实化转译 |
|---|---|
| 发光符文/能量纹/光路 | 删除光效，改为哑光刺绣、金属线绣、提花织纹或皮面压印 |
| 浮空翻领、悬空衣片、飘带 | 加领衬/鱼骨衬/边缘细铁丝/重垂里料，固定于肩腰腕，自然垂落 |
| 金属胸甲、肩甲、铠甲 | 甲片铆接或皮绳穿接在植鞣革底衣与绗缝衬垫上，保留搭接与重量 |
| 皮甲、绑带胸衣 | 厚植鞣革裁片 + 缝线 + 金属搭扣/气眼系带 |
| 薄纱透视装 | 真丝雪纺/欧根纱，标注层数与内衬 |
| 夸张尖肩、几何肩甲 | 垫肩、硬质衬、肩章或轻型甲片，外覆同面料 |
| 魔法变色、荧光渐变 | 真实吊染、渐染、色织渐变或哑光烫印 |
| 宝石、水晶、珠饰 | 树脂/玻璃/金属底座镶嵌，缝或铆在衣片上 |
| 武器、光效、非服装道具 | 不属于服装，不出现（用户明确要求的随身配件除外） |

面料与工艺词库（纤维/织法/做旧）、服装部件词库同 photoreal-garment-plate 的 `references/garment-template.md`，需要时直接调取。

## 七、交付前自检清单

1. 三 figure 与三栏布局正确：左 1/3 正面大头、中 1/3 无头正面、右 1/3 无头背面；无侧面、无第四 figure。
2. 横版 3:2，调用生图时参数同步；提示词写明 horizontal 3:2。
3. 无头裁切措辞完整：顶边在领口上方颈基处、皮肤连续、无断颈/断面/伤口/漂浮头/人台。
4. 连续背景声明与无分割线/无框/无编号/无文字齐全。
5. 面部与 FACE 参考 feature for feature 一致：眼、眉、鼻、颊、颌、下巴、发、配色至少 8 项；媒介否定与 FACE 媒介匹配。
6. 服装逐件写清，材质到纤维与织法；GARMENT 媒介否定匹配；非现实元素已转译或删除。
7. 背面信息：参考图有背面则照写；没有则用成衣常规结构（后片净色、无臆造图案），并在中文说明里告知推断。
8. 正/反两身服装完全一致：颜色、开合、磨损、配件位置与走线（如耳机线两端固定）。
9. 光无影无色偏、无投影、哑光皮肤零油光；85mm、全身自然细节不与大头争清晰；35mm grain 齐全。
10. 全英文、无中文残留，措辞密度与 Golden Sample 一致；结尾含 "Not an artwork… A photograph…"（photo 输入除外）。
